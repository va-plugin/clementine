PART 1 - PROGRAMS INCLUDED WITH CLEMENTINE
------------------------------------------

These are separate programs in the "tools" folder next to Clementine.exe. Clementine starts them as their
own processes; they are not linked into Clementine. Each stays under its own licence, and you may replace
them with other builds (Settings > Tools > Choose...).

PART 3 - PROGRAMS CLEMENTINE USES WHEN THEY ARE ON THIS PC (NOT INCLUDED)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

Clementine starts these programs as separate processes when they are installed. It does not include,
link against or modify them; each is covered by its own licence, which you accepted when installing it.

LibreOffice (optional)
  Use:      PowerPoint to PDF and images, older and OpenDocument formats (.doc, .xls, .ppt, .odt, .ods,
            .odp, .rtf...) and conversions between Office formats. Word (DOCX) and Excel (XLSX) files
            convert without it.
  Licence:  MPL-2.0 (and others for some parts)
  Link:     https://www.libreoffice.org/about-us/licenses/

Microsoft Edge (part of Windows)
  Use:      printing HTML, Markdown, e-books and Clementine's Word and Excel conversions to PDF
            (headless, with a throw-away profile and all network requests blocked)
  Licence:  Microsoft software licence terms (part of Windows)
  Link:     https://www.microsoft.com/edge
